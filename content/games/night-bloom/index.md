Violet, glowing shoots creep toward your houseplant's pot. **Draw a stroke** across the screen and snip them before they get there.

The catch: healthy green shoots grow tangled in among the cursed ones. **Possession** rises while a cursed shoot is still growing, and **Vitality** drops every time you cut a healthy one. If Possession fills or Vitality hits zero, the night is over. Each night is about 30 seconds, and shoots grow faster and tangle more as the nights go on.

## What's in the box

- Daily mode: one shared plant per day, the same for everyone
- Endless mode: keep going for as long as you can last
- Mouse or touch strokes, with a keyboard option (Left/Right to pick, Space to cut)
- English and Korean, switchable in Settings
- Settings for sound, music and reduced motion
- Art and sound are drawn and synthesised in code: the whole game is about 85 KB
- Made for a portrait screen (540x960), so it plays best upright on a phone
