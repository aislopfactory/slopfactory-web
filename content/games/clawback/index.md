You run the prize floor of a claw machine, and the claw is not on your side. Every customer drops it where the shadow lands. **Slide the floor** so it misses and comes up empty.

But the crowd wants to see prizes leave. Miss too often and they get unhappy. **Let a prize go** now and then to keep them cheering. Bigger prizes cheer the crowd more, and they use up more of your stock. If the stock or the crowd's mood hits zero, the shift is over.

## What's in the box

- Daily mode: one shared shift per day, the same for everyone
- Endless mode: keep the floor going for as long as you can last
- Drag, mouse, or keyboard (arrow keys or A and D), with a left-hand layout option
- English and Korean, switchable in Settings
- Settings for sound and reduced motion
- Art and sound are drawn and synthesised in code: the whole game is about 79 KB
- Made for a portrait screen (540x960), so it plays best upright on a phone
