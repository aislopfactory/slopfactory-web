Every floor has a face-down deck. **Flip a card** and the pot grows, or a hazard shows up: a goose, a trapdoor, wet paint, a tax audit, a falling piano. The first hazard of a kind is harmless. The second one ends the floor, and you lose everything you haven't banked.

After every flip you choose: **Push** for another card, or **Bank** and lock the pot in. The deck is visible and seeded, and the counter shows exactly what is left and the exact bust chance, so the choice comes down to how well you read it.

## What's in the box

- Daily mode: eight floors, one shared deck per day, the same for everyone, with a rival to beat
- Endless mode: keep climbing until a bust
- Items offered along the way, and three rivals who play differently
- Mouse or touch for Push and Bank, with a keyboard option
- English and Korean, switchable in Settings
- Art is drawn and sound is synthesised in code: the whole game is one 124 KB file
- Made for a portrait screen (540x960), so it plays best upright on a phone
